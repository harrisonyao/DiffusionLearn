# 🎨 室内装修生成提示词模板（可直接使用）

## 🧩 基础格式
```
[空间类型]｜[主材质]｜[色调光照]｜[风格流派]｜[构图/镜头词]
```

---

## 🏠 Prompt 模版（中英混合增强版）
```
interior design of a [空间类型, e.g. living room / bedroom / dining room], 
featuring [主材质, e.g. wooden floor and glass partition], 
with [色调光照, e.g. warm tone soft lighting], 
in [风格流派, e.g. modern minimalist / Scandinavian / neo-Chinese style], 
captured in [构图/镜头词, e.g. wide-angle view / 45-degree perspective / architectural photography style], 
highly detailed, ultra realistic, natural texture, ray tracing, 8k render, cinematic lighting
```

---

## 💡 示例

**示例 1**
```
interior design of a modern living room, featuring wooden floor and glass wall, 
with warm tone soft lighting, in Scandinavian minimalist style, 
captured in wide-angle perspective, highly detailed, 8k HDR render, cozy atmosphere
```

**示例 2**
```
interior design of a luxury bedroom, featuring marble and velvet materials, 
with neutral beige lighting, in modern luxury style, 
captured with depth of field and cinematic lighting, photo-realistic, ray traced reflections
```

**示例 3**
```
interior design of a dining room, featuring bamboo and concrete textures, 
under natural daylight, in wabi-sabi style, 
architectural photography composition, ultra realistic render, soft focus
```
